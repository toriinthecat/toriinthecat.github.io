Release notes

Newest first.

0.1.0 November 6, 2026

  • First public release.
  • Create and extract password-protected (AES-256) ZIPs, with every password kept in a ledger.
  • Password-protect PDFs.
  • Identify files by their content, not their extension.
  • A command-line version (vault-zip-cli) for automation.
  • The screens are available in Japanese and English (switch it in the settings).
  • A new icon.
  • The portable version can also run with no passphrase.
  • Password fields have an eye icon to show or hide what you typed.
  • A ZIP that holds a single item is extracted without a folder named after it (you can turn this off in the settings).

Update of November 8, 2026 (build 4)

  • Settings are now in 11 groups by what you want to do. On phones you pick a group from a list, and you can search the settings. Settings that are rarely changed are under "Advanced".
  • You can choose, item by item, what the right-click and right-drag menus show (Windows, Linux).
  • The right-drag menu has "Compress each here" (Windows).
  • An archive that contains links (symbolic links and the like) is no longer refused: everything else is extracted, the links are not created, and they are listed.
  • Android: fixed the app sometimes not opening from the share menu. PDF encryption now takes files up to 128 MB.
