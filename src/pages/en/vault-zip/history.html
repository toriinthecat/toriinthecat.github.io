Newest first.

0.1.0 November 6, 2026

  • First public release.
  • Create and extract password-protected (AES-256) ZIPs, with every password kept in a ledger.
  • Password-protect PDFs.
  • Identify files by their content, not their extension.
  • A command-line version (vault-zip-cli) for automation.
  • The screens are available in Japanese and English (switch it in the settings).
  • A new icon.
  • The portable version can also run with no passphrase.
  • Password fields have an eye icon to show or hide what you typed.
  • A ZIP that holds a single item is extracted without a folder named after it (you can turn this off in the settings).
