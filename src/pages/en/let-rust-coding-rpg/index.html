let Rust CODING RPG

An educational app for learning Rust and Go through RPG-style battles, chaptered lessons and flashcards.

Get it on Google Play

What it is

Learn Rust and Go programming as if you were playing an RPG. Ideas that trip people up, such as ownership, borrowing and lifetimes, become weapons, enemies and skills in a world you explore a little at a time.

Read, watch, listen, fight

  • Read: 50 chapters that build up from the basics. Look up a technical term in the dictionary right where you are.
  • Watch: in the Lab, step through how ownership moves and borrowing works in memory.
  • Listen: a podcast that goes over each chapter by voice (more chapters are being added).
  • Fight: quiz battles, and a code-repair boss fight at the end of a chapter. Streaks raise your damage, and bosses change form halfway through.

Your character grows with what you read and solve. Experience, gold, titles and daily missions help you keep going.

Try Go for free

The basics of Go (goroutines, channels, interfaces and more, 5 chapters) can be tried without buying anything.

Who it's for

  • Anyone who wants to learn Rust, even with no programming experience
  • Anyone who has tripped over ownership, borrowing or lifetimes
  • Anyone who wants to keep going a little at a time, in spare moments
  • Anyone who wants to learn in a way that feels like a game
